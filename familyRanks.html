Red → yellow → green shows IV rank percentile from low to high. ★ Best in column has a green border. Dashed blue borders mark recommendations; solid blue borders mark your choices. Click a valid cell to choose it; click again to release it. Duplicate IV spreads count as one Pokémon.

Ratings measure stat product relative to the best IV spread for that Pokémon and league, not strength against other species. CP limits alone do not establish cup eligibility. Family forms and stats come from the repository’s Pokémon data; regional, Mega, and speculative forms retain their labels. Evolution requirements other than Tyrogue’s IV rule are not checked.
